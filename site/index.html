Plynn

Dictation that never touches the internet. Hold a key, talk, and clean text lands wherever your cursor is. Every word is heard, transcribed, and polished on your own Mac.

Download for Apple silicon View on GitHub

What Plynn does

Say it once, get it clean. Fillers are cut, self corrections are applied, spoken lists become real lists, and the tone matches the app you are writing in. All of it on device.

Hold the key. Talk. Release. A live transcript streams into a floating glass capsule while you speak, and the finished text pastes in under a second.

Polish without a server. Apple Intelligence cleans your words on your own silicon. No cloud, no account, no audio leaving the machine.

Instant when it can be. Clean short dictations skip the model entirely and paste immediately. Polish only runs when there is something to fix.

Command mode. Select text anywhere, hold the key, and say what to do with it. "Make this shorter" rewrites the selection in place.

A dictionary that learns you. Fix a word right after a paste and Plynn notices, learns the correction, and gets it right from then on.

Snippets. Say "my email" and your actual address comes out. Any phrase can expand into any text.

A real native Mac app. Swift, SwiftUI, and a menu bar item that behaves. Your dictations live in a local file you can open yourself, and they are sent nowhere.

Meeting notes, on device. Triple-tap to record a call. The transcript and a written summary land as Markdown, produced locally.

History that stays home. Every dictation is kept in local SQLite with your stats: words per minute, words written, time spoken.

Never types into a password. Secure fields are detected and dictation pauses itself until you are somewhere safe.

Your keyboard, your key. fn, Right Option, Right Command, or Right Control. Switching takes effect immediately.

Works with what the mic allows. On a FaceTime or Zoom call, Plynn shares the microphone and keeps working through device changes.

Free and open source. MIT licensed and built in the open, with automatic updates. Read every line before you trust it.
