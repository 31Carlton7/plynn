Hold the key. Talk. Release. There is no app to switch to and nothing to click. Plynn works in every text field on your Mac.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your Mac. Speech recognition, cleanup, your dictionary, and your history all run on your own silicon. No account, no telemetry, no transcript upload.

Instant when it can be

Clean short dictations skip the model entirely and paste immediately. Polish only runs when there’s something to fix.

Spoken formatting

Say “new line”, “comma”, or “press enter” to auto-send. Spoken lists come out as real lists.

Your keyboard, your key

fn, Right Option, Right Command, or Right Control. Switching takes effect immediately.

Works on calls

On a FaceTime or Zoom call, Plynn shares the microphone and keeps working through device changes.

Ready from the first minute

Speech models (about 1 GB) download in the background while Apple’s built-in engine covers your first dictations.

Updates itself

Notarized by Apple and kept current with automatic updates. Built in the open on GitHub.
